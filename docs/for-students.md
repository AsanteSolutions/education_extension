# For students

Everything a student does happens on the **student portal**, the website you
sign in to with the email address the college holds for you. Nothing here needs
the main ERPNext system.

## Registering for your modules

When registration opens you will find a **Registration** page on the portal. It
works in two steps.

### Step 1 — your modules

The page already knows which semester you are due to take. You do not choose it;
it is worked out from what you have passed so far. Each module is listed with a
label telling you where you stand:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Required** | A module for the semester you are entering. Ticked for you and cannot be unticked — your own semester is not optional. |
| **Provisional** | Also required and also locked, but it depends on a result that has not come back yet. See below. |
| **Carried over** | You did not pass this one earlier and it is offered again now. **This is the part you choose.** Ticked by default; untick it if you are not taking it. |
| **Blocked** | You cannot take this yet. The reason is shown on the row. |
| **Passed** | You already have the credit. Nothing to do. |
| **Awaiting result** | An earlier module where you have been granted a supplementary or aegrotat and the result is not back. You are not re-registering for it while that is outstanding. |

So the only real decision is how many carry-overs to take on alongside your own
semester. Blocked modules cannot be ticked at all.

**Why a module might be blocked.** Almost always because a module it depends on
has not been passed. The row says which one. Prerequisites are a hard rule here,
not a warning — if it is blocked, registering is not possible until the earlier
module is passed.

**Why you may not see a module you expected.** Modules are tied to the half of
the year they are taught in. A first-semester module you failed is not shown in
the second semester, because it is not running; it comes back round in the first
semester of next year.

**If you owe fees.** Some colleges switch on a rule that blocks registration
while there is money outstanding. If yours has, the page tells you the amount
and nothing can be ticked until it is settled with the finance office.

### Step 2 — the declarations

Before your registration is final you are shown two things to agree to:

- a **declaration about prerequisites**, confirming you understand the rules
  about the modules you are taking, and
- a **POPIA consent form**, about how the college may use your personal
  information.

Your name, ID number and the date are filled in for you. You sign by drawing
your signature with a finger or a mouse in the box provided.

If a parent or guardian is signing as well, tick **A parent or guardian is
signing as well** and a second box appears for their name and signature. The
system cannot tell whether you are a minor, so this is left to you and the
college to get right.

Once you submit, **your registration is final**. There is no approval step and
nothing to wait for. If something is wrong, speak to the registrar.

## What "provisional" means

Sometimes you are waiting on a result — a supplementary exam that has not been
marked, for example — and that result decides whether you may take the next
module. Rather than making you wait, the system lets you register **provisionally**
and sorts it out when the result lands:

- **The result is a pass** → nothing happens. Your registration simply becomes
  ordinary.
- **The result is a fail** → the module is removed from your registration and
  you are told by email.
- **The result arrives too late, or you have already started the work** → the
  system does not touch it. A member of staff looks at your case and decides.

So a provisional registration is a real registration. You attend, and in most
cases nothing more is said about it.

## Seeing your marks

Marks appear on the **Grades** page of the portal, once the college releases
them for that semester.

This is worth understanding, because it surprises people: **marks do not appear
as soon as they are entered.** A mark goes through checking, moderation and
approval first, and then someone decides the date on which students may see the
results for that semester. Until that moment the page will tell you that results
have not been published yet, even though your lecturer finished marking weeks
ago. That is deliberate — it stops half-finished or unmoderated marks being
read as final.

Once released, you will see a row per module with four columns:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Course** | The module. |
| **DP** | Your semester mark, built from your tests, assignments and practicals. |
| **Final Mark** | The DP and the exam combined, in the proportions set for that course. |
| **Remark** | A short code saying what the outcome was. |

If you wrote a supplementary, two more columns appear — **Supp Exam** and
**Supp Remark** — so both the original attempt and the re-sit stay visible.

The codes are explained in the [glossary](glossary.md). The ones you are most
likely to see are **P** (passed), **PD** (passed with distinction), **C**
(condoned — a 49% that has been allowed to pass), **F** (failed) and **SUPP**
(you have been granted a supplementary exam).

A supplementary result is published separately from the main one, so it is
normal for your main results to appear first and the supplementary columns to
follow later.

## Your other pages

The portal also carries your timetable, attendance, fees and leave applications.
Those come from the standard education system rather than from this add-on, and
work as they always have.

## Privacy

You can only see your own records. Another student's marks, contact details or
registration are not reachable from your account, whether through the portal or
otherwise.
